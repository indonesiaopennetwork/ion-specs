# B2C-LIVE — Happy Path

The BPP publishes a time-bounded live offer. A consumer enters through a live,
OTT, social, or attributed-content surface and selects the offer while it is
valid. The BAP records the source channel and available attribution fields. The
BPP returns a quote and, when enabled, a queue position. The consumer completes
`init` and `confirm` before the reservation expires, after which the order follows
the standard physical-goods fulfilment states.

See [`pattern.yaml`](../pattern.yaml) for the complete step-by-step requirements.
