# Release 1 Trade flows

Release 1 includes one validated reference flow:

- [`storefront/v1`](patterns/storefront/v1/) — a standard B2C purchase of a
  packaged physical product, from catalog publication through delivery.

The other root Trade patterns and variants remain non-normative work in progress.
They were not copied because several still depend on excluded common packs,
Logistics extensions, or the excluded `ion.yaml` aggregate. Omitted flows may be
reviewed for a future integer release.

The Storefront pattern uses the nine Release 1 Trade packs and the included
Address, Business Registration, Payment, and Tax common packs. Its schema and
context references are release-qualified and remain inside Release 1.
